## Compass

- This started as a project to further explore what Apple Intelligence can do, and evolved into an analytics app.
- The app provides simple analytics metrics.
- The app utilizes Apple Intelligence on iPhone/iPad to distill specific insights about the data. When Apple Intelligence is not available, it defaults to template insights.
